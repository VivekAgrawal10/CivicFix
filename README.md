# CivicFix
Hackathon
So we are making a website 'CivicFix'
basically people will report problems on the website like person x saw a pothole or waterlogging on road A then they will click its picture and upload it on the app under categories
each report will be check by 2 means
1- has any other person reported the same problem
2- the location on the picture taken ie both persons location shall be same and pothole should look similar
but if in some village only 1 person reports problem then to solve that issue we will divide areas locality wise like with locality with low users only 1 report with genuine photo checked by ai is enough

then severity of the problem will also be detected by the ai on our website with ML like all potholes with diameter bigger than 3m comes under severe category and the routes of people travelling will also be rerouted on our website
yes our website will also give routes to people going form location x to y
then moderate or low severity problems will not reroute.
but also severity of locaties with hospitals and schools shall always be high priority.

also every reported and cofirmed report will be stored in our database which will be shared with some repair company and they will go and fix the issue and update on our database that its done
also the busy routes problems shall be given higher priority
also if road B gets frequent waterlogging problems then it will be saved in its history and ai will detect the reoccuring problem and a feature called AI suggestions will recommend that maybe there is some drainage issue at the place and inform it to the repair company

every locality or road will have a road health score eg 38/100 for a road where potholes occured 3 per month and 2 per month drainage issues

also in places where it waterlogs frequently, we will know that by road's history , when it rains the road shall be marked with logging prone area , so weather app shall also be integrated
